# Gullak AI: 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **Setting the Stage:** Open Gullak AI in full screen. Ensure sound is unmuted for the Paytm Soundbox 4G morning briefing and voice interaction. Language set to Marathi (or Hindi/English).

---

## Act 1: The Problem & The Merchant Mental Model (0:00 – 0:45)

**Presenter:**
> *"Judges, Paytm put 10 million Soundboxes in Indian shops. But today, once a payment is made, the Soundbox goes silent. The shopkeeper still struggles alone with festival surges, lost customers, and cash shortages.*
> 
> *Meet Ramesh Sharma, owner of Sharma General Store in Dadar. Diwali is exactly 9 days away. Ramesh doesn't need 19 complicated enterprise analytics dashboards. He thinks in 5 simple questions:*
> 1. **आज (Aaj):** What happened today and what 3 things must I do right now?
> 2. **विचारा (Vichara):** Can I just speak to my assistant in Marathi or Hindi?
> 3. **क्रिया (Kriya):** What actions are waiting for my approval?
> 4. **ग्राहक (Grahak):** How do I bring back my customers?
> 5. **माल आणि पैसा (Maal & Paisa):** Will I run out of stock and how much money do I have?*
> 
> *This is Gullak AI — the AI partner a shopkeeper trusts with their money."*

---

## Act 2: The Hero Story & 1-Tap Approvals (0:45 – 1:45)

**Action:** Show the **आज (Aaj)** surface.

**Presenter:**
> *"Look at Ramesh's home screen. No financial jargon, no RFM matrices. 
> 
> Right at the top: Today's sales are ₹40,717. Tomorrow's bank deposit is clearly split between online settlement and cash in his till — with our strict guardrail: never guessing ₹0 if bank data is pending.
> 
> And look at this header: **'गुल्लकमुळे या महिन्यात अतिरिक्त नफा: +₹14,520 थेट मोजलेला'** — Gullak helped earn ₹14,520 extra this month, and it is strictly measured, not hallucinated.
> 
> Below are the **Top 3 Actions Today**, ranked by expected rupee impact:*
> 1. **Stock Warning:** Desi Cow Ghee will run out on Thursday before Diwali Dhanteras. Gullak has auto-drafted an ONDC order for 45 Litres with wholesale supplier Mahalaxmi Dairy.
> 2. **Customer Revival:** 60 loyal customers haven't visited in 30 days. Gullak has drafted a personalized WhatsApp Diwali greeting with an 8% discount code.
> 3. **Working Capital:** A ₹1,00,000 festive inventory credit line with instant daily deduction.
> 
> *Notice: Gullak NEVER executes on its own. It acts as an advisor. Ramesh taps **'मंजूर करा (Do it)'** right here with one tap."*

**Action:** Tap **"मंजूर करा (Do it)"** on the Desi Ghee card. Card updates instantly with a green checkmark!

---

## Act 3: Closed-Loop Impact & Tamper-Evident Trust (1:45 – 2:30)

**Action:** Click **क्रिया (Kriya)** in the sidebar, then click the **"निकाल (Results)"** tab.

**Presenter:**
> *"Every AI company claims they 'increase sales'. But how do you prove it?
> 
> Gullak implements **closed-loop causal uplift with a 20% holdout control group**. 
> When we ran the WhatsApp campaign to 60 lapsed customers, 48 were sent the message and 12 were withheld as a control group.
> 
> Look at the plain-language result:
> **'६० ग्राहकांना संदेश पाठवला, २१ परत आले (३५%). संदेशाशिवाय फक्त ९ (१५%) आले असते. तुम्ही सुमारे ₹८,२४५ अतिरिक्त कमावले.'**
> 
> *If the sample size is too small, Gullak shows 'Too early to tell' rather than inventing numbers.*
> 
> *Now, why would a shopkeeper trust AI with their supplier orders?*
> *Under 'Advanced', look at our **Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Audit Trail**. Every action is cryptographically chained to Ramesh's biometric or one-tap consent. Let's click 'Verify Integrity' — all 50 sequential blocks verified valid. If an attacker or rogue script modifies a rupee value, the chain immediately breaks and flags an alert."*

---

## Act 4: Paytm Soundbox 4G & Indic Voice (2:30 – 3:00)

**Action:** Click the green **"साउंडबॉक्स 4G सक्रिय"** badge in the navbar to open the Soundbox Modal. Click **"आवाज ऐका (Play Soundbox)"**.

**Soundbox Audio Speaks:**
> *"शुभ प्रभात रमेशजी! आज ₹40,717 विक्री झाली. 8 वस्तूंचा साठा कमी आहे. दिवाळीआधी ऑर्डर मंजूर करा."*

**Presenter:**
> *"Every morning at 8:00 AM, the Paytm Soundbox speaks a concise briefing under 40 words, and drops a clean WhatsApp summary on Ramesh's phone.
> 
> And if Ramesh asks in Marathi:*
> (Speak or type into Vichara): **'आजची विक्री किती झाली?'**
> *The assistant responds with today's exact ₹40,717 grounded sales in pure colloquial Marathi.*
> 
> *Switch to Hindi or English, and the entire app, voice synthesis, and recommendations adapt seamlessly.*
> 
> *Gullak AI: It speaks their language, explains itself, asks before acting, and keeps a receipt for everything. Thank you!"*
